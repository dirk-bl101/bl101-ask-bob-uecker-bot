BFCM BEST PRACTICES (page 1 is an image; text below transcribed from the page image)
Retail, Black Friday / Cyber Monday 2026. Know the rules of the sale so every shopper gets a win.
BLACK FRIDAY RETAIL PRICING: Mon Nov 23 to Mon Nov 30. No Cyber Monday-specific deals in Retail.
Key dates: Nov 23 retail pricing starts. Nov 26, 8 PM: online sale starts. Nov 27: Black Friday. Nov 30: last day of pricing.
1. THE RETAIL PRICING WINDOW. Retail pricing runs Monday, Nov 23 through Monday, Nov 30. Share the dates early. Great talking points with customers starting now.
2. BEST SELLERS SELL OUT. New, best-selling and limited edition items go fast. Customers need to purchase while in store; we cannot guarantee stock next time. No restocks during the sale, and no price matching.
3. NO CM-SPECIFIC DEALS. No Cyber Monday-specific deals or online CM pricing in Retail. Black Friday sales stay active in Retail through Nov 30.
4. ONLINE IS SEPARATE. The online sale (up to 50% off site-wide) starts Nov 26 at 8 PM EST, with VIP early access on Nov 25.
5. PRICING, CODES AND POINTS. Sale pricing reflects at ring-up. No additional codes are valid, including Loyalty & Rewards. Points accrue but cannot be redeemed until the sale is over.
6. BUNDLES ARE DISABLED. Bundles are disabled for the duration of the sale, including the $10 promo tee and the 3/$75 and 5/$100 tee bundles. CX will not price match, regardless of whether the shirts are on sale.
7. RETURNS AND EXCHANGES. Extended, rolling 60-day window for Nov 1 to Dec 20 purchases. Example: bought 11/3, return or exchange by 1/3. EXACT item only: returns and exchanges for the EXACT item, size change only, are honored at the sale price once the sale completes. If it is not the EXACT item, the sale price does not roll over. Traveling? Customers can process their return/exchange online once they are home.
QUESTIONS? ASK IN THE RETAIL-CX SLACK. Follow all SOPs. If you are unsure, do not process it; reach out.
Customers can reach us at info@BL101.com, live chat, 631-649-7133, M-F, 10 AM-4 PM EST.
