SOURCE: "SOP: Tagging a Sale in Shopify" (Google Doc / file supplied by Dirk Armstrong).
TOPIC: Adding a tag (for example FBVIP) to a Shopify order in the website admin for tracking and reporting.

Purpose: make sure all qualifying sales are tagged in Shopify for tracking and reporting.
Step 1, log in (website only): open a web browser (Chrome, Edge, etc.), go to the Shopify website login page, and log in with your store email credentials. Do NOT use the Shopify app.
Step 2, go to Orders: click Orders in the left-hand menu.
Step 3, find the order: use the search bar at the top of the Orders page, type the order number, press Enter, and click the correct order.
Step 4, add the tag: scroll down the order page; on the right-hand side find the Tags section; click into the Tags field; type the code; click Add.
Step 5, confirm: make sure FBVIP appears under Tags before you leave the order. Nothing more is needed once the tag is added.
Important notes: always do this in the Shopify website in a browser, not the app. Double-check the order number before adding the tag. Enter tags exactly as given: all caps, no spaces.
