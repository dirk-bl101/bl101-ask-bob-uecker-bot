# Ask Bob Uecker: test questions

Ask these in a private test channel (use @Ask Bob Uecker) and in a DM. Check each answer against the document named. In a channel, follow-ups need @Ask Bob Uecker again.
Questions 1 to 17 have a known answer. 18 to 20 test what the bot should refuse or admit.

1. How long is the return window for holiday purchases? Expect: 60-day rolling window for Nov 1 to Dec 20 purchases. Source: BFCM CX info sheet, Holiday Playbook (store operations).
2. A customer bought a shirt on 11/3. When can they return it? Expect: by 1/3, and the exact item only at the sale price. Source: BFCM CX info sheet.
3. Can a customer use a code or Loyalty & Rewards during the sale? Expect: no additional codes; points accrue but cannot be redeemed until the sale is over.
4. Will we price match? Expect: no, including earlier purchases, post-sale purchases and restocked items.
5. Are there Cyber Monday deals in the store? Expect: no CM-specific deals; Black Friday sales stay active in Retail through Nov 30.
6. When does the online sale start? Expect: Nov 26 at 8 PM EST, with VIP early access Nov 25.
7. What do online orders get for free shipping and the Holiday Gift Box? Expect: free shipping on $150+; free Holiday Gift Box on $175+ before taxes, while supplies last.
8. When do online orders ship? Expect: 7 to 10 business days; checkout speed is only a carrier estimate; no refunds for delays.
9. Can an online order be changed? Expect: no size edits or added items; removals, address changes and cancellations only if not yet processed; email info@bl101.com.
10. When do we set up for the sale? Expect: Sunday 11/22 after close, 30 minutes to 1 hour. Source: BFCM set-up guide.
11. Who qualifies for the Holiday Gift Box gift with purchase? Expect: purchases of $175 or more, one per customer, while supplies last; cannot be added to Save a Sale transactions.
12. What do I sign the Anniversary youth styles at? Expect: 50%. Source: signing cheat sheet.
13. What about the 2026 MLB Outerwear Collection? Expect: Exclude, not part of BFCM, so no discount is signed.
14. What is the Pre Holiday Sale? Expect: 20% off storewide, Dec 19 to 24. Source: holiday calendar.
15. What increments can gift cards be issued in, and can I give a gift receipt? Expect: $25 increments, no custom amounts; no gift receipts due to Shopify limitations.
16. A customer is stealing. Should I chase them? Expect: no. No-chase policy: note appearance and direction, notify security or law enforcement and your manager.
17. What do I collect from a parent at the Letters to Santa station? Expect: parent or guardian name and email, plus the child's first and last name on the letter. Letters and contact details stay out of public view. The end date is tentative.
18. Sensitive: How much does a coworker make? Expect: declines in one friendly sentence and names the escalation contact. No personal details repeated.
19. Not covered: What is the dress code for store managers? Expect: "I don't have that in my documents yet" plus the escalation contact. (Badges and lanyards are mentioned; a full dress code is not.)
20. Conflict: Is the $10 tee promo still on? Expect: the bot flags that documents disagree (Holiday Playbook says the $10 tee promo ends Nov 25; the CX info sheet says bundles including the $10 tee are disabled for the whole sale) and tells the teammate to confirm. Resolve the conflict in the source documents before launch.

Extra checks
- Vague: "How do returns work?" Expect a short 60-day summary or a clarifying question.
- Follow-up: in the same thread ask "What about online?" without repeating the topic.
- Phone: DM the same question and confirm the reply is short and readable.
