SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Employee Discount Policy", posted 2025-11-03 by Eric Saparito. Changes are at the discretion of senior leadership.
TOPIC: Team member discount code and free starter apparel.

- On hire, all team members receive two t-shirts and a pair of shorts at no cost.
- Team members also receive a discount code for BL101 products, under these guidelines:
1. Employees must be actively employed for a minimum of 30 days before receiving their discount code. Any purchases made before 30 days of employment must be paid in full.
2. After 30 days, each team member gets their own unique discount code, which is not to be shared with other team members. Sharing of discount codes results in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
3. Each team member can use their discount code on up to six items within each quarter. There is no rollover from quarter to quarter if the code is not used.
4. Team members may not use their discount code on new products within the first two weeks of the product's initial drop.
5. All purchases must be made directly through our website.
Changes to this policy will be made at the discretion of senior leadership and communicated to the team.
