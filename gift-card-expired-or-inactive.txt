SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Gift Card Issues: Expired or Inactive", posted 2026-06-30 by Kristin Keys. Applies to: Store Managers, ASMs, Key Holders.
TOPIC: Gift cards that show as expired or inactive at point of sale.

Purpose: Provide a process for handling gift cards that show as expired or inactive at point of sale.

Steps:
1. Do not tell the customer the gift card is invalid. Advise them that the team is checking on it.
2. Collect: the gift card number (photographed or written down) and the customer's phone number or email on file.
3. Post in #retail-store-cx with: Store, Issue (gift card showing expired / inactive), Gift card number or photo, Customer contact (phone or email).
4. CX will locate the gift card in Shopify and reinstate it if eligible.
5. Once reinstated, CX will confirm the current balance. Share the balance with the customer.

After-hours: If CX is unavailable, advise the customer that the team will follow up the next business day and collect their contact info. Post in the channel and tag Dirk Armstrong for awareness.
