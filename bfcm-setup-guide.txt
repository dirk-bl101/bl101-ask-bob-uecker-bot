BL101 · UP TO 50% OFF SALE

Black Friday & Cyber Monday 2026
Promotional set-up summary for store teams

The promotion: a tiered “Up To 50% Off” sale with four price tiers (25%, 35%, 40%, 50%). Stores set up on the
evening of Sunday 11/22, after close, so signs are live when the sale starts. The sale runs Monday 11/23
through Monday 11/30.

=== set-up guide page 4 ===
• Windows: clean glass, place the cling at the most visible height (move mannequins if needed), keep it
straight and bubble-free.
 • Tables and fixtures: acrylic stands on each table (GWP sign if not on sale). Put 40% and 50% items on
rounders or high-capacity fixtures. Never tape signs to fixtures.
 • Hanging signs (KOP, Riverhead, Roosevelt Field): one holder per 4 ft wall section, each sign labeled with
its tier. Sign mixed-tier collections at the LOWEST tier.
 • Fitting rooms (ALL stores): 8.5"×11" sign in an acrylic holder on each door, with the Holiday GWP sign.
 Placement examples (pricing/signage in the photos may not match current promotions; use the
promo guide):

25% off | 35% off | 40% off | 50% off | Stanchion Sign

 Utilize Pant Hangers if needed to add extra signage(should be used primarily on the
walls)

Pricing, Walkthrough and Upkeep
 • Pricing: every sign matches the current tier; check SKUs, exclusions and bundles; pull inactive promotions
(tee bundle and Loyalty, store in a safe place), ticket all merchandise.
 • Signage: Signs should be IMPACTFUL throughout the store. Every table, fixture, and wall section (within
what makes sense in promo brackets) should be signed.
 • Walkthrough: The Store Manager checks placement, price messaging, sightlines, hanging signs and fitting
rooms before opening on Monday 11/23, then submits at least 8 photos to their store’s leadership chats.
 • Daily: straighten signs, replace damaged ones, and regroup price points as items sell through.

=== set-up guide page 5 ===
• After the sale (end of day Mon 11/30): GWP signs stay up through December (Remove once sold out).
Save tier signs in store until told otherwise. Discard window clings.

Big Key Points
 • Monday the 16th to Friday the 20th - stores should have visuals moved and prepped leading into the
weekend.
 • Managers should plan to arrive in store an hour early to verify the store is set up and all marketing is set
prior to store opening.
 • The window banners will stay after the Black Friday Sale through Movie Drop Setup in December.
 • Utilize pant hangers for wall signage on fixtures.
 • GWP will need to be rung up with purchases (this includes the gift box and wrapping paper)

Store Set Up Checklist
11/16-11/20
Remerchandise the store as needed to support promotional setup. (Refer to Cheat Sheet
Guide for Reference). Keep this simple and move what will be most impactful.
All Managers read the Black Friday Setup Guide AND BFCM Promo Cheat Sheet
11/20/2026- Have all Sale signage printed out from staples, office max, fedex, etc. (Do not print on
your stores printer)
11/22/2026 (After Close)-
All bundle signage and new customer signage take down
All zones are signed correctly under the correlating discount
Black Friday Window cling has been put up
Stock Bounceback Coupons at Each Register
Starting 11/23/2026- Review in Huddles with Store team throughout the week and validate team is
aware of promotions and guidelines within the promo.

Store Breakdown Checklist
11/30/2026 (After Close)
Remove all BFCM sales signs from the floor (25, 35, 40, 50% signs) - These will be put in a safe
area in back rooms. DO NOT DISCARD until directed.
Put back all bundle and new customer signage (areas visible for what coordinates within
regular promos; cash wrap, fitting rooms)
Window clings will be pulled down; window banners will remain till the end of December.
12/1/2026 (Before Opening):
Opening Manager validates completion of checklist above and that no signage was missed.
Update merchandising as needed to support the end of promotion.
