SAFETY AND EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
CODE ADAM (MISSING CHILD)
When a child goes missing, make a Code Adam announcement so every employee can begin a discreet search
right away. Check specific areas of the store, alert security, and contact local authorities if needed.

ACTIVE SHOOTER: RUN, HIDE, FIGHT
Alert law enforcement with the store location and details, and activate the alarm so employees can start the
procedure. Guide customers calmly to safe areas or exits.

 RUN | HIDE | FIGHT
 If a safe exit is available, evacuate | If you cannot evacuate, find a | Last resort only. If confronted
 immediately. | secure area, lock or barricade | directly, be prepared to defend
doors and stay silent. | yourself.

After an incident: account for everyone, cooperate with authorities, and debrief and support the team.

NO-CHASE POLICY
Never physically pursue or detain a suspected shoplifter. Note the person’s appearance, clothing and direction
of travel, notify security or law enforcement, and alert your manager without escalating.

EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
Each mall location receives a packet from mall security with all emergency contacts. Download Simon Connect
for live updates from inside the mall. At a baseball facility, follow the facility’s protocols.
