SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Safety Procedures" (Standard Operating Procedure), posted 2025-11-03 by Eric Saparito.
TOPIC: Code Adam (missing child), active shooter, lockdown, post-incident actions, no-chase policy, emergency procedures.

Code Adam: a missing-child alert procedure. When a child goes missing in the store, a Code Adam announcement is made so that all employees can immediately begin a discreet search. Predefined actions include checking specific areas of the store, alerting security, and contacting local authorities if necessary.

Active shooter procedures: emergency plans to protect employees and customers. Key elements:
- Alert law enforcement: quickly contact emergency services with the store's location and details.
- Activate the alarm: trigger internal alert systems so employees are informed and can initiate the procedure.
- Follow Run, Hide, Fight. Run: if a safe exit is available, evacuate immediately. Hide: if evacuation isn't possible, find a secure area, lock or barricade doors and remain silent. Fight: as a last resort, if confronted directly, be prepared to take action to defend yourself.

Employee responsibilities
- Training and drills: regular training on recognizing threats and understanding roles.
- Customer assistance: guide customers calmly to safe areas or exits, avoiding panic.
- Communication: maintain clear and discreet communication with team members and, if applicable, a designated incident command center within the store.

Lockdown procedures
- Secure the premises: lock all entrances and exits to contain the threat.
- Designated safe zones: identify and mark safe areas where employees and customers can shelter in place.
- Avoid confrontation: do not attempt to engage the shooter unless absolutely necessary and there is no other option.

Post-incident actions
- Account for everyone: once safe, conduct a head count.
- Cooperate with authorities: provide information and follow their instructions.
- Debrief and support: offer psychological support and review the procedure to address gaps.

No-chase policy: employees must not physically pursue or detain individuals suspected of shoplifting or other suspicious behavior. Purpose: employee safety, liability management, and focus on observation (note appearance, clothing and direction of travel for law enforcement). Key components: observe and document discreetly without direct engagement; notify security personnel or law enforcement immediately; communicate with colleagues and management without escalating. Implementation: regular training, signage and reminders in store, coordination with security.

Emergency procedures
- Each mall location is given a packet from mall security with all contacts to call in case of an emergency inside the mall.
- Download Simon Connect for live updates inside the mall.
- At a baseball facility, follow the facility's protocols in case of an emergency.
