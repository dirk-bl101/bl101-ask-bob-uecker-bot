SOURCE: "iPad / POS Troubleshooting Guide" (IT Best Practice SOP, v1.0; Google Doc / file supplied by Dirk Armstrong). Newer than the Slack canvas "IT Best Practice". The shared iPad login PIN was removed from this copy; ask your Store Manager for it.
TOPIC: Fixing register offline, pin pad, scanner, store music, printer and internet problems; who to contact.

Register offline:
1. Disconnect the device from Shopify POS.
2. In the iPad Bluetooth settings, disconnect the device.
3. Go back into Shopify POS and reconnect the device. This fixes it in most cases.
If it persists: find the small reset button on the back of the device, reset it, and reconnect through Shopify POS again.
Pin pad issues: reset the Tap & Chip reader through Bluetooth before resetting it directly in Shopify. The iPad login PIN is shared (ask your Store Manager). Shopify PINs are store-created and can vary by location.
Scanner disconnects: press the scanner power button; disconnect and reconnect the scanner in Bluetooth settings; reconnect it within Shopify POS if needed.
Store music not playing: reconnect the music device/system through Bluetooth; make sure the store Wi-Fi is connected properly for Sonos.
Printer issues: make sure receipt paper is loaded correctly; clear any paper jams; disconnect and reconnect the printer through Bluetooth if needed.
Internet down: use a mobile hotspot if available; use the store MiFi device if available; if it persists, contact the Granite Wi-Fi provider for the store location.
Escalation (non-emergency): contact via Slack or email Amanda@BL101.com and HumanResources@BL101.com.
