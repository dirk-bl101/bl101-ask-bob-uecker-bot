SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Supplies / Staples Account". (No login details are included; use your store credentials.)
TOPIC: Ordering store supplies from Staples.

1. Log in to Staples with your store credentials.
2. Use the BL101-approved store supply list (Google Sheets). Select it as your store list. If you need supplies outside the approved list, ask on Slack to confirm first.
3. Add items to the cart and submit the order. Orders over $75 require a receipt and an explanation.
4. Share a weekly Google Sheets log with your DM/DOR (Amanda@BL101.com).
