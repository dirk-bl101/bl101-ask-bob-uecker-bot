SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "New Store Opening Guide" (v1.0; template fields blank). Audience: Store Managers, Assistant Managers, New Store Opening Coordinators.
TOPIC: New store opening: store types, hiring timing, setup-week schedule, backstock, safety, merchandising, soft/grand opening.

Store types: Retail (full-price stores with the latest collections); Micro (full-price locations in baseball tournament facilities, may be seasonal); Outlet (outlet, warehouse stores and tent sales; older SKUs, samples, slow movers); Pop-Up (temporary spaces tied to marketing/promotional events).
Hiring timing before opening: Store Manager 3-4 weeks; Assistant Manager (Process) 2-3 weeks; Assistant Manager (Product) 2-3 weeks; Assistant Manager (People) based on volume; Key Carrier 1-2 weeks; Sales Associates about 1 week. Screen applicants quickly; first interview can be by phone, second in person; use the BL101 Sales Associate Interview Guide.
Setup-week staffing: Mon 4-5 associates incl. management; Tue 4-5; Wed 7-8 (if space allows); Thu 7-8; Fri grand opening 4-5 depending on volume.
Training covers: customer service and selling, POS use, store layout and product knowledge, inventory receiving, visual merchandising standards, safety and loss prevention.
Setup week:
- Monday: take possession, walk through and note issues; swap keys and cores; receive supplies and fixtures; Wi-Fi install; set up supplies; set up labeled, organized backstock.
- Tuesday: finish supplies; build fixtures (if not construction's scope); set fixtures to the New Store Floor Map; report broken/damaged/missing fixtures to your Director of Retail.
- Wednesday: receive merchandise and validate against the TO order confirmation; process and sort by collection; begin merchandising per the Visual Merchandising Guide.
- Thursday: finish sales floor merchandising and send pictures to the Director of Retail; set the most recent window directive and send pictures; Thursday afternoon soft opening if the store is ready and approved.
- Friday: grand opening.
Essential equipment: POS (iPad, stand, card reader), Wi-Fi, music system, indoor and outdoor signage, DOR traffic counter.
Backstock: organize shelves by collection, then product type, then size. Older merchandise can go in large boxes stacked safely. Talk to your DM about overstock.
Safety: keep backroom exit paths clear; do not stack boxes over 5 ft and use the T method; review ladder, box-cutter and general safety with all associates at orientation. Keep a designated damaged-items area; tell your DM when you have full boxes of damages.
Visual merchandising: group by collection except Clearance. Clearance is separated by discount level (10%, 20%, 30%) then by size using size rings on every fixture. Weekly drops go at the front by Thursday morning; move product toward the back as it ages. Check signage, price tags and promo displays daily. Keep 36 inches of ADA clearance on all customer pathways and never block egress. Store extra hardware, racks and fixtures in the backroom.
Merchandising areas: window displays, Drop Zone (front, newest collection), feature wall (new arrivals/seasonal), clearance zone (separate but organized).
Pre-opening checklist: fixtures installed; inventory received, steamed, tagged and merchandised; staff trained; signage installed; soft-opening run-through done.
Soft opening: invite family, friends and loyal customers; test POS, staffing and inventory flow; gather feedback. Grand opening: promotions (for example first 50 customers get a gift), influencer or local media, discounts or bundles, social countdown, capture photos and video.
Post-opening: staff debrief after week one; review sales and feedback; adjust schedules and displays; implement loyalty and email capture; keep weekly team meetings.
