SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Facilities Damage & Merchandise Loss Reporting". Applies to: Store Managers, ASMs.
TOPIC: What to do when roof leaks, water damage or other facilities issues damage the store or merchandise.

Purpose: a clear process for documenting and reporting physical damage to the store or merchandise caused by facilities issues (for example roof leaks, water damage).
1. Safety first. If there is an active leak or structural hazard, move merchandise and associates away from the affected area immediately.
2. Document the damage. Take photos of all affected areas and damaged merchandise. Count and record the number of damaged items, including style name, size and color.
3. Notify mall management immediately if a building issue (roof, ceiling, pipes) caused it. Get the name of the mall contact you spoke with.
4. Post in the facilities channel (#facilities) with: Store; Date/time discovered; Type of damage (roof leak / water damage / other); Merchandise affected (number of items, style details); Mall notified (yes / contact name); Photos.
5. Contact your District Manager / VP of Retail for next steps on merchandise write-off or reimbursement.
6. Facilities will coordinate with mall management on the structural repair.
Notes: do not discard damaged merchandise until authorized. Damaged items may need to be held for insurance or claim documentation.
