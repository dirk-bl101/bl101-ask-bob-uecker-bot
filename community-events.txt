EVENT BLACKOUT DATES
Do not schedule events on these dates. They fall in peak sales
periods, so our priority is everyday sales and store
performance.

NOV 25 – 30 | Thanksgiving Peak

DEC 12 – 13 | December Peak Weekend

DEC 19 – 31 | Peak Holiday Season

BEST PRACTICES
Plan ahead. Shift event scheduling to slower-traffic
January and February.

Build relationships. Ask guests how their fall went or if
they are looking forward to baseball season. | Use the holiday season to build relationships that
drive store traffic in the new year.
Find event leads. Connect with local teams, coaches and
league organizers who may host events.

Prioritize holiday sales. Avoid overcommitting to events
during peak shopping periods.

Stay organized. Keep the floor clean, stocked and
customer-ready on busy weekends.
