SAVE THE SALE

Endless Aisle SOP & Store Training Guide

Brand Initiative: Save the Sale

Purpose: The Save the Sale program empowers our stores to never lose a customer due to inventory limitations. When an item isn’t available in-store, we use Endless Aisle tools to complete the sale, make a family happy, and ensure the store still receives credit toward their All Pro Bonus.

This is not just a process — it’s a mindset.

WHY SAVE THE SALE MATTERS

-   We keep families happy – no disappointment, no “we don’t have it” moments.

-   We protect revenue – every saved sale counts.

-   We reward the store – the sale credits back to the originating store.

-   We build trust – customers see us as problem-solvers, not gatekeepers.

Golden Rule: > If the customer wants it, we find a way to get it to them.

PHASE 1: POS-BASED ENDLESS AISLE (LIVE NOW)

PHASE 1 SOP – SAVE THE SALE USING SHOPIFY POS

WHEN TO USE ENDLESS AISLE

Use Save the Sale when: - A size, color, or style is out of stock in-store - The store does not carry that item - Inventory is available at another store or the warehouse

Do NOT say: - “We’re out.” - “We don’t have that.”

Instead say: > “I can save this sale and ship it to you.”

ASSOCIATE RESPONSIBILITIES

-   Own the customer experience

-   Stay with the customer through checkout

-   Confirm shipping details

-   Ensure the order is completed in POS

STEP-BY-STEP: SAVING A SALE IN SHOPIFY POS

1. START THE SALE

-   Open Shopify POS

-   Log in with your staff credentials

-   Add the desired product to the cart

2. IDENTIFY INVENTORY ISSUE

-   If the item is out of stock at your store, do not stop the sale

3. SELECT ALTERNATE FULFILLMENT LOCATION

-   In the cart or order screen, choose Fulfill from another location

-   Select:

    -   Warehouse or

    -   Another store with available inventory

  No store setting changes are required.

4. CONFIRM CUSTOMER DETAILS

-   Verify:

    -   Shipping address

    -   Contact information

    -   Any special notes

5. COMPLETE CHECKOUT

-   Process payment in POS

-   Confirm order completion

-   Reassure the customer: > “Your order is all set — we saved it for you.”

IMPORTANT PERMISSIONS & ACCESS

To use Endless Aisle in POS, staff must: - Have POS access - Have permission to: - View multiple locations - Assign fulfillment locations

Managers should have full permissions by default.

  If access issues arise, notify leadership immediately.

MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES (CRITICAL)

Managers are the drivers of adoption.

Managers must: - Lead by example — use Save the Sale daily - Coach associates in real-time - Reinforce the bonus credit benefit - Ensure staff never turn customers away due to inventory

Manager Checklist: - [ ] All staff trained - [ ] POS access verified - [ ] Save the Sale language used consistently - [ ] Endless Aisle orders reviewed weekly

CUSTOMER LANGUAGE (USE THIS SCRIPT)

-   “We can ship that directly to your home for free.”

-   “I’ll take care of that for you right now.”

-   “You won’t leave empty-handed today.”

This creates confidence and trust.

ALL PRO BONUS – HOW CREDIT WORKS

-   The sale is credited to YOUR store

-   The associate and store benefit exactly like an in-store sale

-   Saving sales directly impacts bonus performance

  Bottom line: Saving the sale = winning for everyone.
