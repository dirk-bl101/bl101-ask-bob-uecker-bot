SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Customer Service Best Practices", posted 2025-11-03 by Eric Saparito.
TOPIC: Greeting, engaging and serving customers; manager on duty expectations.

Customer Service Best Practices
1. Greet EVERY customer. Acknowledge every customer with a warm greeting as they enter the store.
2. Engage and educate. Provide product knowledge, recommend items, and connect with customers through shared passion for baseball. Start with something simple: "Welcome to Baseball Lifestyle 101! Have you shopped with us before?"
   - If no: "We started as a simple Instagram page created by a baseball-obsessed player and have grown into one of the biggest baseball lifestyle brands. What began as a small community turned into a company that now supplies major sporting goods stores and connects players and fans who live and breathe the game. Our apparel is designed to represent the passion and culture of baseball both on and off the field."
   - If yes: "Awesome! We just dropped a brand new collection if you would like to check that out. Is there anything specific you came in for?" (If applicable: "Also, we've added some great items to the clearance rack if you're looking for a deal.")
3. Maintain a positive attitude. Be approachable, energetic and solution-oriented.
4. Prioritize customer needs. Offer assistance proactively and handle inquiries with care.
5. Encourage upselling and cross-selling. Recommend complementary products based on customer interests.
6. Ensure a clean and organized store. Keep the sales floor tidy, replenish merchandise, and maintain an inviting atmosphere.
7. Handle transactions efficiently. Process sales smoothly, follow up on online order pickups, and thank customers for their business.
8. Address customer issues promptly. Resolve concerns with professionalism, escalate when necessary, and ensure customer satisfaction.

Manager on Duty (MOD) and floor supervision expectations
- Lead by example. Set the tone for excellent customer service.
- Monitor store activity. Ensure associates are actively engaging customers.
- Delegate tasks. Assign responsibilities for floor coverage, fitting room assistance and restocking.
- Maintain security awareness. Prevent shrink by staying vigilant and following loss prevention procedures.
- Reinforce company values. Uphold Baseball Lifestyle 101's brand image and community-driven approach.
