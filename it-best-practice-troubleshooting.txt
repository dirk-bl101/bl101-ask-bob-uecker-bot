Troubleshooting common issues
- Register offline: reset the tap and chip on Bluetooth first, before resetting via Shopify.
- Pin pad: the iPad login PIN is shared by stores (ask your Store Manager). The Shopify PIN is store-created.
- Scanner disconnects: press the power button and reset via Bluetooth.
- Phone line down: submit an issue through the Level 10 portal on the Baseball Lifestyle 101 Hub and notify your DM.
- Store music not playing: reconnect via Bluetooth.
- Printer issues: make sure paper is loaded and clear any paper jams.
- Internet down: use a hotspot or MiFi if possible. If the issue persists, call the Granite Wi-Fi provider the store is connected to.
