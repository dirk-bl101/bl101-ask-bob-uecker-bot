BL101 Shopify POS Training Guide
New Associate Step-by-Step
STEP 1: Create Your POS Account
Before using the POS, every associate must create their own account and PIN.
1. Open the Shopify POS app.
2. Select Staff.
3. Select Add.
4. Enter your:
   * First Name
   * Last Name
   * Phone Number
   * Email
   * PIN
5. Your PIN must be 4–6 digits.
6. This PIN will be used to log into Shopify POS.
Important: Never share your personal POS PIN with another associate.
________________


STEP 2: Add Products to the Cart
If the item has a tag:
1. Scan the item's barcode/tag using the POS scanner.
2. The product will automatically populate in the cart.
3. Confirm the item and quantity are correct.
If the item does NOT have a tag:
1. Use the search function on the POS.
2. Type in the product name or identifying information.
3. Find the correct product.
4. Add it to the cart.
5. Double-check that you selected the correct item.
Always make sure the product, size, color, and quantity are correct before completing the transaction.
________________


STEP 3: Add a Discount Code
Discount codes are entered through the POS Home Screen.
1. From the Shopify POS Home Screen, select the three dots (•••) in the top right corner.
2. Select Apply Discount.
3. Enter the discount code required for the transaction.
4. Confirm that the correct discount has been applied before completing the sale.
Important
Most discount codes cannot be used more than once.
If a code has already been used and you attempt to use it again, you will receive an error message:
“Code has been used.”
Do not try to bypass the system or reuse a code that has already been used.
________________


STEP 4: Save-A-Sale Discount
If a customer uses WELCOME20 or another 20% discount code and then wants to complete a Save-A-Sale or separate purchase afterward:
Use:
SAS20%
This is used to match the customer's original 20% discount.
Important: SAS20% should only be used when appropriate to match the original qualifying discount. Do not abuse or use this code for unrelated purchases.
________________


STEP 5: Retail Bags
Every transaction where a BL101 Retail Bag is handed to the customer MUST have the bag added to the POS.
1. Look at the POS tiles.
2. Select Retail Bag.
3. Choose the correct size:
   * Small
   * Large
4. Make sure the bag is included in the transaction.
If you hand out a retail bag, it must be accounted for in POS.
________________


STEP 6: Create a Gift Card
Gift cards can be created as either a Digital Gift Card or a Physical Gift Card.
To Start:
1. At the top of the POS, search “Gift Card.”
2. Select BL101 E-Gift Card.
3. Choose between:
   * Digital Gift Card
   * Physical Gift Card
Digital Gift Card
1. Select Digital Gift Card.
2. Choose the amount needed.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Confirm the email is correct.
5. The gift card will be sent directly to the customer's email.
Always double-check the email address before completing the transaction.
Physical Gift Card
1. Select Physical Gift Card.
2. Choose the amount needed.
3. Use the iPad camera to scan the barcode on the back of the physical gift card.
4. The scan will populate the gift card information.
5. Confirm the correct amount and gift card information before completing the sale.
Important: Make sure the physical gift card is properly scanned and attached to the transaction before completing the sale.
________________


STEP 7: Gifts With Purchase (GWP)
If a customer qualifies for a Gift With Purchase:
1. Add the qualifying purchase to the cart.
2. Add the appropriate GWP item to the cart.
3. The GWP should ring up as $0.
4. Confirm the GWP is showing at $0 before completing the transaction.
Do not manually discount the GWP. Add the designated GWP item to the cart and allow POS to process it correctly.
________________


POS Golden Rules
Scan it when you can. Search it when you can't.
Double-check every item before payment.
Apply discounts correctly.
Account for every retail bag.
Add qualifying GWPs to the cart at $0.
Double-check gift card information before completing the transaction.
Never share your POS PIN.
