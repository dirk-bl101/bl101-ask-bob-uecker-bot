TOPIC: Rewards / loyalty program in Shopify POS (Okendo Loyalty): enrolling customers, viewing profiles, redeeming and applying coupons.

The Baseball Lifestyle Rewards Program rewards our most passionate fans for shopping online and in store. It is powered by Okendo Loyalty and built into Shopify POS.
Customer enrollment:
1. Start a cart in POS and tap "Add customer".
2. Search for or create an account (include the email!).
3. Tap the Okendo Loyalty tile and hand the device to the customer.
4. The customer accepts the terms and joins. They are now enrolled.
5. Make sure they are enrolled in SMS and email marketing.
Viewing a loyalty profile:
1. Tap "Add customer" and select a member.
2. Tap the Okendo Loyalty tile to see email, points, VIP tier and rewards.
Redeeming rewards:
1. The Redeem tab shows eligible coupons only.
2. It filters by points, cart, tier or subscription-only rules.
Applying coupons:
1. Go to the "Coupons" tab in the loyalty profile.
2. Select and apply previously redeemed coupons to the cart.
FAQs and best practices:
- Combine discount plus product only (GWP).
- Loyalty points behave the same for refunds and returns.
- No separate POS coupons are needed.
- Staff: always verify emails, promote signups, and know the rewards and VIP tiers.
