SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "SOP 8 - Monthly Shopping Event Booking". Applies to: Store Managers, ASMs.
TOPIC: Booking the required 3 shopping events per month; timeline, outreach, reporting.

Minimum requirement: 3 Shopping Events per store per month (drives traffic, community partnerships and incremental revenue).
Timeline:
- By the 1st of each month: confirm at least 1 event is already booked for the upcoming month.
- By the 15th of each month: all 3 events should be booked and on the calendar for the following month.
- Ongoing: keep up outreach to build a pipeline of future partners.
How to book an event:
1. Review the Retail Community Partnerships spreadsheet for existing contacts and past partners.
2. Use the BL101 Cold Call Guide for outreach scripts when contacting new organizations.
3. Use the BL101 Shopping Event Guide for event structure, setup and expectations.
4. Target youth sports teams, school athletic departments, travel baseball/softball organizations and local community groups.
5. Confirm event date, time, group name and expected attendance, then add it to the store's event calendar.
Monthly reporting: when you answer the monthly event check-in in the marketing channel, include the teams/organizations you have confirmed or are talking with, event dates (confirmed or tentative), and any obstacles to booking so leadership can help.
Reminder: shopping events are proven revenue drivers and a required part of community engagement.
