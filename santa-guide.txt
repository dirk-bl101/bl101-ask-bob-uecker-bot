2. Set up the station
Choose the location: Pick a prominent, easy-to-see location where families can write comfortably
01
without blocking shopping paths or exits.

02 | Prepare the table: Set up the 6 ft table securely, add the tablecloth, and wipe the surface clean.

03 | Place the mailbox: Position the mailbox securely where kids can easily drop off their letters.

04 | Arrange writing supplies: Set paper and pens neatly on the table, leaving clear space for writing.

Install marketing materials: Use the final reference images when provided. Do not treat the concept
05
diagram in this guide as a substitute for final marketing placement instructions.

Brief the team: Walk the team through participation steps and talking points before inviting families to
06
join.

3. Help families participate

1
Invite kids and their parent or guardian to visit the station and write a wish list.

2
Let kids know their wish list can include anything they would love to receive—not just BL101 items.

3 | Before placing the letter in the collection box, collect the parent or guardian's name and email address.

4 | Invite the child to drop the completed letter into the holiday mailbox. Thank the family for joining in.

SET THE RIGHT EXPECTATION
Create Christmas magic and fun while setting clear expectations with families about the letter submission process.

4. Talking points for the team
“ Have you written your letter to BL101 Santa yet? Make your wish list right here and drop it in
our holiday mailbox. Let's send a little baseball magic to the North Pole!

“ What would you love to find under your Christmas tree? Add anything you’re wishing for to
your postcard—your list doesn’t have to be just BL101 items!

“ Parents and guardians, help BL101 Santa keep every wish list organized! Please include your
child’s first AND last name, along with your name and email address, on the letter.

“ Drop your letter in, make a Christmas wish, and who knows what BL101 Santa might leave
under your tree!
