SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Submitting a Work Order".
TOPIC: Reporting broken or needed-replacement items in the store (repairs and maintenance).

Submitting a work order
1. Initiate: if something breaks or needs replacing ASAP, reach out to your DM/DOR directly on Slack.
2. Complete the required fields: Store Location; Class (choose "Repairs & Maintenance"); Description (a detailed description of the issue); Photos (attach images using your tablet). Be as specific as possible about what is needed and give required measurements or specifications.
Tracking status
1. Once the issue is communicated to the DM/DOR you will hear back about next steps.
2. If a replacement item is ordered, an order number will be provided (if applicable).
3. Follow the tracking/order number and tell the DM/DOR when the item arrives.
