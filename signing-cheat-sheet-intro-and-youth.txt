BFCM 2026 Retail Signing Cheat Sheet
Nov 11, 2026 | · | @Amanda

Important: Confirm the style before you sign it, and pay close attention to 2025 vs
2026 collections. Several collections have both a 2025 and a 2026 version (for
example Ice Cream and Ice Cream 2026, Cotton Candy and Cotton Candy 2026,
Baseline and Baseline 2026), and the discounts are not the same.

How to use this sheet
Sign the youth price for every collection in store. Part 1 lists, by collection, which discount
bucket each youth and all-ages style (hats, socks, bottles, backpacks, patches) belongs
to. Adult pricing is separate in Part 2 and is for reference only, so shoppers see one price
on the sign. "Exclude" means the style is not part of BFCM, so no discount is signed.

At a glance
This sheet covers 1,668 styles across 115 collections. 828 are youth or all-ages styles (sign
these) and 840 are adult styles (reference only).

Bucket | Youth and all-ages styles | Collections | Adult styles

50% | 334 | 76 | 490

40% | 87 | 19 | 57

35% | 81 | 18 | 36

25% | 220 | 44 | 146

Exclude (not in BFCM) | 106 | 5 | 111

Not in BFCM, whole collection: 2026 MLB Outerwear Collection, Holiday 2026 Part 1,
MLB, MLB All Star Collection 2026, MLB Collection September, Mystery and NYFD 2026
are all Exclude. Holiday II and Colorblock Pullover 2026 are also Exclude. Neither has rows
in Part 1 or Part 2. A few other styles are excluded inside collections that otherwise run a
discount, and they show as "Exclude" in Part 1.

not listed here, see Part 2.
