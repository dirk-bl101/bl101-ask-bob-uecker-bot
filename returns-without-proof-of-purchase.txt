SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Returns Without Proof of Purchase (No Order Number)", posted 2026-06-30 by Kristin Keys. Applies to: Store Managers, ASMs, Key Holders.
TOPIC: Returns when the customer has no order number, receipt or account information.

Purpose: Provide guidance on handling return requests where the customer has no order number, receipt, or account information.

Step 1: Verify the item is BL101 merchandise
1. Attempt to scan the item's tag or barcode in Shopify POS.
2. If the item does not scan, inspect the tag closely:
   - BL101 tags include our branding and are distinct from other retailers.
   - Items purchased at Dick's Sporting Goods or other retailers cannot be returned to BL101.
3. If you are unsure whether the item is ours, photograph the tag and item and post in #retail-store-cx for confirmation before proceeding.

Step 2: Look up the customer
1. Search the customer's name, email address, or phone number in Shopify to locate their order history.
2. If a matching order is found, proceed with the return using the located order number.

Step 3: If no order can be located
1. Do not process the return without authorization.
2. Post in #retail-store-cx with: Store, Situation (customer requesting return, no order number, item was a gift), Item description (style, size, color), Tag scanned in POS (yes / no / did not scan), Customer contact (phone or email if provided), Photo of item and tag.
3. CX or a manager will advise on whether to proceed with a store credit, exchange, or decline.

Policy reminder: We are not able to process returns for items purchased at third-party retailers, even if the product is BL101 branded. When in doubt, escalate. Do not make an exception without manager approval.
