GUEST EXPERIENCE

HOLIDAY GUEST STANDARDS
Every guest, every area, every basket.

1 | GREET EVERY GUEST
Welcome everyone as they come in, even in a rush, and let
waiting guests know you see them.

2 | ENGAGE IN EVERY AREA
Do not stop at hello. Work the whole salesfloor: walls, tables,
accessories and the register line. Ask who they are shopping
for and what the occasion is.

3 | BUILD THE BASKET
Style full outfits and add accessories that finish the look.
Show how pieces work together so one item becomes a
complete gift or outfit. | Every guest is a family shopping for a
tradition.

4 | SUPPORT THE FITTING ROOM
Offer a room, check in, and bring other sizes and styles.
Suggest pieces to complete the outfit while they try it on.
THE GOAL
Every guest leaves with a
complete outfit, the right
5 | SIGN UP FOR LOYALTY AT THE REGISTER
accessories, and a loyalty
Invite every guest to join loyalty before they pay, with one
sentence on the benefit. All guests should be enrolled in | sign-up.
loyalty for both email and SMS, and the Accepts Marketing
toggles must be ON for both new and existing guests so
every guest receives all BL101 communications. Goal: 90%.

6 | FIX IT ON THE FLOOR
Use Save A Sale (Endless Aisle) when a size or style is not in
the store, so the sale does not walk out the door.

7 | KNOW THE PRODUCT AND PROMOTIONS
Refer to Product Knowledge sheets for each collection with
your team. Leverage Promotional Guides and Reference
sheets to familiarize all team members on seasonal promos.

8 | USE APPEASEMENT BOXES WITH CARE
The 10 set-aside Holiday Gift Boxes are for Customer
Appeasement.

DE-ESCALATION AND SUPPORT
Stay calm, solve it, and know when to bring in help.

DE-ESCALATE IN FIVE STEPS

Slow your voice and relax your posture.
1 | STAY CALM | Do not take it personally.
“Let’s take a breath. I’m here to
help.”

Let them finish. No interrupting or
2 | LISTEN | defending. Repeat back what you heard.
“Tell me what happened.”

Name the feeling, then own the next
3 | EMPATHIZE | step.
“I understand why that is
frustrating.”

Offer what you can do now. Be honest
4 | SOLVE | about what you cannot.
“Here is what I can do right now.”

If it is not resolved, bring in the SM or
5 | ESCALATE | ASM and move away from the crowd.
“Let me bring in my manager so
we can fix this.”

Safety first. If a guest is threatening or the situation feels unsafe, step away, keep your team and
guests safe, and call mall security or 911.

Need support? For transaction or order issues, contact the CX team in the Retail-CX channel on
Slack. Partner with Amanda, Director of Retail, on any customer appeasement issues that can’t be
handled at the store level.
