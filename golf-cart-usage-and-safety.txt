SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Golf Cart Usage and Safety Guidelines".
TOPIC: Golf cart rules at micro locations.

Usage: only authorized drivers may operate golf carts. All operators must be trained and review the Golf Cart Usage SOP. Establish clear routes for moving items between off-site storage and the main facility. A BL101 associate must always remain at the container; never leave it unattended.
Safety: do not exceed seating capacity or the cart's rated cargo capacity. Passengers stay seated while moving, with arms, legs and feet inside. Drive at safe speeds, especially around turns, pedestrians and hills; slow down in crowds and on wet or uneven ground. Park on a level surface, set the parking brake, and remove the key and secure the cart when unattended.
