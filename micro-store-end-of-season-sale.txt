SOURCE: Slack #sop-stores canvas "Micro Store End of Season Sale" (v1.0; template fields and promo codes blank in the original).
TOPIC: Micro location end-of-season sale: setup, signage, promotions, marketing.

Goal: clear as much backstock as possible ("everything must go"); secondary goals are traffic, content/customer engagement and a high-energy brand experience.
Required store setup:
- Schedule staff at least a week out. All staff onsite for the event, as many as needed for prep the day before. Work start is 3 hours before the sale starts. Need more time? Talk to your DM.
- Extra POS: at least 1 additional POS station (mobile or tablet), 2 total. If you lack one, talk to your DM.
- Create a rough layout and submit it to the DM.
- All backstock out on racks or in totes; coordinate racks with your DM. All merchandise priced. Decide what goes on racks versus totes (highest sellers on racks).
- Set up a table outside the container for easier checkout and more merchandise.
Signage: exterior yard signs, easels, A-frames near venue entrances, inflatable "SALE" tube man next to a yard sign in view of traffic; two 22x28" signs in front of the container (25% off whole store). Interior: laminated rack signs on both ends of racks; raffle sign by the box at the outside POS (6.2x4" acrylic holder; raffle closes at 3PM). Facility: yard signs with sale details on main roads, 6 ft sale flags. Required signage list: wavy inflatable tube man; 22x28" event rules sign; 6.2x4" raffle sign; 8x11" flyers; 24x36" A-frame; 24x36" yard sign. If you lack materials, contact leadership ASAP. Extra hangers and generic bags are ordered and sent directly to the store.
Promotions (in-store only): 25% off storewide (a given code, shown as a tile on the Shopify app homepage; can be combined with a mailer code). Mailer codes: for customers who bring in the mailer; mailers are collected when redeemed; can be combined with the storewide code. Spend $100 = 1 raffle entry to win a $150 BL gift card; winners are chosen, contacted and paid out by the Store Manager. Putting green and mini-games are optional.
Vibe: upbeat playlist created by the SM; managers drive team motivation and promo engagement; staff speak to promotions, upsell raffle tiers and encourage social tagging.
Marketing: emails start 2 weeks before; mailers to past customers nearby; marketing creates countdown graphics and promo callouts; stores hand out printed flyers to local businesses and sport facilities and post in local "Baseball Moms" Facebook groups.
